iso-factories

Animated isometric factory simulations. Each factory is one self-contained page that runs in the browser.

Factories

  • Northside Works, Line 4

    A packaging line drawn from about 2,700 boxes a frame. Two depalletizing robots share a conveyor without ever crossing; widgets are painted, dried, tested and packed, and autonomous forklifts move pallets between the line and the dock. Machines break down, a tech fixes them in priority order, and the crew takes tea breaks.
